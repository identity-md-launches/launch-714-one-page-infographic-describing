HOW POOL4 WORKS

explained by pepes armed with AI

a self-running Uniswap v4 pool that trims, burns, rewards & defends its own token

EXAMPLE POOL
Fake World Assets ($FWA) / ETH
Uniswap v4 · Ethereum mainnet
hook 0x087a…CA840
1

YOU SWAP

Buy or sell FWA for ETH like any Uniswap pool. A 1% fee pays the protocol. The hook runs only after your trade settles, so your quote never changes.

2

THE HOOK CHECKS THE STASH

The pool may only hold so many tokens (the cap). If sells push FWA over the cap, the hook pulls the extra out, evenly on both sides, so the price doesn't move.

FWA right now: 844K in the pool, cap is 986K, so nothing to trim yet.
3

85% BURN · 15% REWARDS

Trimmed tokens go two ways: 85% are burned (sent to 0x…dEaD forever), 15% become rewards. By default the 15% splits into stakers (4.5%), bonds (6%) and AI inference nodes (4.5%).

4

ETH BUY WALL

The ETH pulled out during trims becomes a floor bid under the price. If FWA dips into it, the wall buys. No new tokens are ever minted: it's recycled ETH. Keepers then rebalance it for a small tip.

5

STAKE & CHILL

Deposit tokens, get vault shares. Rewards drip in slowly and steadily, so each share is worth more tokens over time. Bonds turn their 6% into ETH that pays for AI compute.
